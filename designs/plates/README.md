# Plates and gasket pads

All current plate files are under [`plate/`](../../plate/). They are split
left/right **1.5 mm POM** files with four PCB-matched M2 holes per half, two
outer and two centre-side gasket tongues per half, and at least 2.01 mm minimum
web width.
